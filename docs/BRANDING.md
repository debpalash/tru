# Tru brand

## Purpose

AI can accelerate research. It cannot replace reporting or establish truth by
assertion. Tru brings people back to original sources, independent coverage and
clear distinctions between evidence and inference.

**Find the facts. Keep your privacy.**

Tru is free to use, open source and independent. It has no subscription, ads,
behavioral analytics or developer-operated data collection endpoint. Optional AI
uses a private service selected by the reader; third-party policies still apply.

## Identity

The logo is the word **Tru**, set in the bold editorial forms of Source Serif 4
Black. The lettering itself is the mark. No checkmark or badge promises that a
story is true. Ink (#171717), white (#ffffff) and gray (#606060) keep reporting
central. The app uses native system text for accessible reading and controls.

The launcher uses white lettering on an ink field. Keep the whole word inside
the adaptive-icon safe area. Splash screens use the same wordmark. Android
status-bar alerts use a compact editorial T so the symbol remains legible.

Source Serif 4 is licensed under the SIL Open Font License. Original font and
license: assets/brand/. The logo assets are outlined SVGs, so rendering does
not depend on an installed font. See scripts/generate-wordmark.py (FontTools),
then bun run brand:assets (librsvg and ImageMagick) to reproduce the assets.

## Voice

Use short, direct sentences. Link to evidence. Say when sources disagree.
Describe corroboration as corroboration, not a truth guarantee. Explain what
leaves the device before a reader connects a service. No inflated claims about
AI accuracy, anonymity or third-party retention.

## Screenshots

Capture the real release app on an isolated emulator with no personal account.
Use current public reporting. Keep tokens, service origins, cookies and private
messages out of images. Store screenshots and demos in docs/screenshots/ and
docs/demos/; copy unframed PNG screenshots to Fastlane metadata.
